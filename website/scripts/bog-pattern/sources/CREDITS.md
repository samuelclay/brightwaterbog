# Silhouette credits

Animal and carnivorous-plant silhouettes are from [PhyloPic](https://www.phylopic.org),
fetched via its public API and recolored by `../gen.py`. Mountain laurel and blueberry
are drawn in the generator (PhyloPic has no *Kalmia*, and its *Vaccinium* loses the
berries at the size we use).
